How to play

New here? Start with the Gentle intro book: small Beginner puzzles that teach the rules one at a time. Tap the book name above the board to switch books.

Draw one single closed loop along the grid lines passing through every circle (white and black), with no crossings or branches.

  • White circles (○): the loop must go straight through the circle, and must turn 90° at least one step immediately before or after it along the loop.
  • Black circles (●): the loop must turn 90° at the circle, and must go straight through both adjacent intersections along the loop (at least two segments straight in each direction).
  • Empty intersections may or may not be visited by the loop.

Controls

  • Tap a grid segment to cycle: empty → line → ✕ → empty.
  • Drag along the grid to draw (or, starting on an existing line, erase) loop segments; retrace backward mid-drag to backtrack.
  • Right-click a grid segment to toggle a ✕ directly, or right-drag to swipe-place/erase ✕ marks across multiple segments.
  • Tap or drag across the centers of squares to shade them as Inside or Outside the loop (left-click cycles blank → Inside → Outside → blank; right-click cycles in reverse; dragging continues painting or erasing the same color).
  • Hint shows the next human-style deduction (rule, tier, cost, explanation, and ghost marks). Press it again to cycle through other firings of the same rule; click anywhere to dismiss. See the illustrated solving guide for examples of every rule Hint knows.
  • ← / → move between puzzles. Ctrl/⌘-Z undo, Ctrl/⌘-Shift-Z redo. Esc cancels a drag.
  • Circles highlight red when a rule is broken (can be toggled in Settings, along with Auto-X, Auto-Shade, and line colors).
