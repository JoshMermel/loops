How to play

Draw one single closed loop along the grid lines visiting every circle, with no crossings or branches. Empty intersections are optional.

White circle (○)

Go straight through, and turn 90° immediately before or after (or both).

Black circle (●)

Turn 90° at the circle, and go straight for at least 2 segments both ways.

Controls

  • Draw / erase lines: drag along grid lines (or tap a segment to cycle line / ✕ / blank).
  • Place ✕ marks: right-click or right-drag along grid lines.
  • Shade squares: tap or drag across squares to mark Inside / Outside.
  • Need a nudge? Press Hint for the next deduction, or browse the solving guide.
