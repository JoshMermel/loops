How to play

Draw one single closed loop along the grid lines visiting every circle, with no crossings or branches. Empty intersections are optional.

White circle (○)

Go straight through, and turn 90° immediately before or after (or both).

Black circle (●)

Turn 90° at the circle, and go straight for at least 2 segments both ways.

Gray circle (special rule)

Secretly either a white or black circle — figuring out which is part of the puzzle. Tap a gray circle to label it white or black as an optional note.

Hamilton cycle

The loop must visit every intersection dot — none may be left empty.

Swiss cheese holes (special rule)

Cutout holes cannot be entered. Every hole sits entirely Inside or entirely Outside the loop — tap anywhere in a hole to shade it.

Slitherlink numbers (0–3)

Each number inside a square tells how many of its 4 surrounding grid sides belong to the loop. Tap any square (even a numbered one) to shade it Inside or Outside.

Mid-loop dots (teal)

The loop must pass straight through every teal dot, and each dot must sit at the exact midpoint of its straight segment — equal length on both sides up to the 90° turns. Dots on intersections sit on even-length runs (2, 4, …); dots on segment midpoints sit on odd-length runs (1, 3, …).

Castle Wall tiles (white / black / gray)

The loop cannot enter blocked Castle Wall tiles. White tiles must sit strictly inside the loop, black tiles strictly outside, and gray tiles may be either. Each numbered arrow counts the total length of parallel loop segments in that direction all the way to the board edge (looking past other tiles).

Controls

  • Draw / erase lines: drag along grid lines (or tap a segment to cycle line / ✕ / blank).
  • Place ✕ marks: right-click or right-drag along grid lines.
  • Shade squares: tap or drag across squares to mark Inside / Outside.
  • Label gray circles: tap a gray circle to mark it white or black (right-click cycles in reverse); labeling is optional.
  • Shade a hole: tap anywhere inside a Swiss cheese hole to shade the whole hole Inside / Outside.
  • Need a nudge? Press Hint once to see where to look, or twice for the full deduction — and browse the solving guide.
